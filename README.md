<h2 align="center">About Me</h2>

I'm **Tanmay Sharma**, a Full-Stack Developer with **3+ years of professional experience**, focused on designing, developing, and deploying production-oriented software.

I work primarily with **React, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, and modern backend technologies**. My experience spans the complete development lifecycle — from frontend architecture and API engineering to database design, third-party integrations, deployment, and infrastructure.

Beyond development, I'm interested in product engineering, system architecture, and applied AI automation. I enjoy solving practical business problems through reliable and maintainable software.

- Building full-stack applications and business platforms.
- Designing scalable APIs and database architectures.
- Integrating payments, authentication, and third-party services.
- Working with cloud infrastructure and deployment automation.
- Exploring AI-powered workflows and product engineering.

Currently building and contributing to software initiatives at **Gradmarc Adtech LLP**.
